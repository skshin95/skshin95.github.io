Research area / 03

Scientific AI Integration

Accelerating high-dimensional, physics-based reactor models through physics-informed neural networks, geometry-aware surrogate modeling, and computational optimization.

PINNs Geometry generalization Surrogate modeling Optimization

Approach / 01

Physics-informed neural
networks for CFD

A deep neural network learns flow fields from both data and the governing physical equations, keeping its predictions consistent with the underlying physics. As a result, the PINN reproduces CFD flow fields more closely than a conventional neural network.

Approach / 02

Surrogate-based
optimization

Detailed reactor models are replaced by DNN surrogate models trained on their predictions, enabling fast process optimization that iterates design variables until the output matches the target.

Applications

Application / 01

Reactor blade design using geometry-aware PINN

Geometry-aware PINNs that predict flow fields across blade geometries, enabling simultaneous optimization of reactor design and operating conditions.

Application / 02

Multiphysics PINN for an ethylene polymerization reactor

A multiphysics PINN predicts velocity, pressure, temperature, and species concentrations from reactor operating conditions, trained with continuity, Navier–Stokes, and species balance losses alongside CFD data. It reproduces CFD fields and generalizes beyond the training range more accurately than a conventional neural network.

Application / 03

Polymerization reactor optimization for target MWDs

A DNN surrogate trained on 3,000 CFD-derived molecular weight distributions maps operating conditions (initiator, chain transfer agent, and temperature) to MWD shape, enabling optimization of a nonideal polymerization reactor for target MWDs.
