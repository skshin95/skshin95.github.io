02 / Research

From Molecules to Reactors,
Driven by Automation and AI

Multiscale modeling built on physics-based computational methods.

My research focuses on computational foundations, automation, and AI for chemical process engineering. I connect molecular chemistry, transport phenomena, and reactor-scale dynamics through multiscale modeling. I develop scientific AI methods to integrate these scales efficiently for process prediction and optimization.

RESEARCH AREA / 01 Explore research ↗

Automated Reaction Network Modeling

Building detailed kinetic models for fuel systems with the Reaction Mechanism Generator (RMG), based on quantum chemistry databases.
RESEARCH AREA / 02 Explore research ↗

Multiphysics Modeling of Chemical Reactors

Coupling flow, transport, reaction kinetics, and population balances to predict product distributions.
RESEARCH AREA / 03 Explore research ↗

Scientific AI Integration

Physics-informed learning, geometry-aware surrogates, and AI-assisted tools for faster simulation and design.
