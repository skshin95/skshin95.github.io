Research area / 02

Multiphysics Modeling of Chemical Reactors

Predicting reactor performance through coupled models of fluid flow, mixing, transport, reaction kinetics, and evolving product distributions.

CFD Population balance equations Polymerization Molecular weight distributions

Approach / 01

Coupling transport and reaction across scales.

Industrial reactor behavior depends on how chemical reactions interact with local transport and mixing. My work combines computational fluid dynamics, compartmental models, and population balances to connect complex reactor physics to measurable polymer properties and process outcomes.

Explore overall research framework ↗

Approach / 02

CFD compartment model
(chemical reaction network)

CFD flow fields are divided into compartments that form a network of ideal CSTRs, connected through a flow network matrix. This enables efficient coupling of chemical reactions with non-ideal mixing.

Applications

Application / 01

Polymerization reactors

CFD-coupled kinetic and population balance approaches for predicting molecular weight distributions in nonideal, high-pressure polymerization reactors.

Application / 02

Pharmaceutical precipitation

Mechanistic population balance modeling of lipid nanoparticle (LNP) formation, capturing nucleation, growth, and coalescence to predict particle size distributions.
